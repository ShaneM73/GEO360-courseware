SLR / DSLR

Optical viewfinder
Composing: mirror down, sensor covered

Light is reflected upward by the 45° mirror, through the pentaprism and into your eye. The imaging sensor is not yet receiving the scene.

Mirrorless

Mechanical shutter
Composing: sensor continuously active

Light goes straight through the lens to the image sensor. The sensor continuously produces frames that are sent electronically to the EVF or rear LCD.

Live viewTriggerEVF interruptedReadoutLive view resumes
Optical light pathElectronic video signalSensor actively receiving / integrating light
Key idea: the SLR must physically move its mirror out of the optical path before the sensor can see the scene. A mirrorless camera has no reflex mirror; its sensor already sees the scene during composition. During a long single exposure, the sensor is integrating the photograph, so its normal live-video feed to the EVF is interrupted and resumes after exposure/readout.
