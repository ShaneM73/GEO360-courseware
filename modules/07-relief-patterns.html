What students should notice

1. Radial directionEvery top image is displaced directly away from PP along its own radial line.
2. Distance from PPAt the same h and H, vertical objects farther from PP have larger d.
3. Object heightIncreasing vertical-object height h increases the separation between base and top images.
4. Flight heightIncreasing H moves images inward and reduces relief displacement across the entire photo.
5. Terrain variationIf object heights vary randomly, the displacement pattern becomes irregular. In mountain or uneven terrain, this makes relief displacement harder to recognize and harder to remove.
