How the calculation works: For a standard 9 × 9 inch aerial photo at average scale 1:N, the ground length covered by one photo side is 9 in × N. If the required forward overlap is p, then the overlap amount is (9 in × N) × p. The distance between two exposure stations (air base / photo distance) is therefore (9 in × N) × (1 − p). The interactive 3D scene below illustrates exactly that idea and updates live when you adjust the demo overlap.
